Andhra Pradesh IAS Officer Transfers - June 2026

The Andhra Pradesh government executed a significant bureaucratic reorganization involving 18 IAS officers through Government Order No. 1100, issued June 22, 2026, by Chief Secretary Neerabh Kumar Prasad.

Key Personnel Changes:

S. Nagalakshmi transitioned from Vizianagaram District Collector to Guntur, replacing M. Venugopal Reddy, who was directed to the General Administration Department (GAD).

B.R. Ambedkar moved from Director of Mid-day Meals and Schools Sanitation to Vizianagaram District Collector.

A. Mallikarjuna (Visakhapatnam District Collector) was directed to GAD.

A.S. Dinesh Kumar was appointed Alluri Sitharama Raju District Collector, succeeding M. Vijaya Suneetha.

A. Thameem Ansariya progressed from Srikakulam Municipal Corporation Commissioner to Prakasam District Collector.

Sagili Shan Mohan relocated from Chittoor to Kakinada District, replacing J. Nivas.

C. Naga Rani advanced from Technical Education Director to West Godavari District Collector, succeeding Sumit Kumar (reassigned to Chittoor).

K. Vetriselvi transitioned from Women Development and Child Welfare Director to Eluru District Collector, replacing V. Prasanna Venkatesh (directed to GAD).

P. Prasanthi moved from Agriculture Marketing Director to East Godavari District Collector, succeeding K. Madhavi Latha.

Srijana Gummalla became NTR District Collector, replacing S. Dilli Rao.

P. Ranjit Basha assumed Kurnool District Collector duties from Bapatla.

Additionally, Peeyush Kumar, Additional Secretary of Commerce, was repatriated to his parent cadre.
